Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2, 2026

Introduction

Book a Cook ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: We collect minimal data necessary to provide the app's functionality. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Email address (if you sign in with email)
  • Name (if provided via Apple Sign In)
  • Unique user identifier for authentication

Usage Data:

  • Cookbooks you add to your library
  • Recipes you save from TikTok
  • Ingredients you scan and track

Images:

  • Photos you take for ingredient scanning are processed by AI but not stored on our servers
  • Images are only sent to OpenAI for processing and are not retained

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • To personalize your experience with recipe recommendations
  • To process your subscription payments (via Apple)
  • To communicate with you about app updates or support

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): For user authentication and analytics
  • RevenueCat: For subscription management
  • OpenAI: For AI-powered ingredient recognition
  • Groq: For recipe extraction from videos

Each of these services has their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

Data Storage & Security

  • Your cookbook and recipe data is stored locally on your device
  • Authentication data is securely managed by Firebase
  • We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission
  • We do not store images on our servers

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Book a Cook is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
