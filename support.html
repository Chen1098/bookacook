❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a cookbook? ▼
Go to the Books tab and tap the + button. You can scan the barcode on your cookbook or search by title. We have over 30,000 cookbooks in our database!
How do I save recipes from TikTok? ▼
  1. Open TikTok and find a cooking video
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Select "Book a Cook" from the share options
  4. We'll automatically extract the recipe from the video!
Tip: This works best with videos where the chef clearly explains the recipe. Videos without verbal instructions may not work.
How do I scan my ingredients? ▼
Go to the Kitchen tab, select a category (Fridge, Pantry, or Freezer), and tap the camera button. Take a photo of your fridge or pantry, and our AI will identify the ingredients automatically!
What's included in Pro? ▼
Free users get:
  • 1 cookbook per month
  • 3 recipe imports per month
  • 3 ingredient scans per month

Pro users get:
  • Unlimited cookbooks
  • Unlimited recipe imports
  • Unlimited ingredient scans
How do I restore my purchase? ▼
Go to Settings (gear icon) > Upgrade to Pro and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.
Why isn't my barcode scanning? ▼
Try these tips:
  • Ensure good lighting
  • Hold the camera steady
  • Make sure the entire barcode is visible

Note: Not all cookbooks are in our database. If scanning doesn't work, try searching by title instead.
Recipe extraction failed. What should I do? ▼
Recipe extraction works best when:
  • The video has clear audio
  • The chef verbally explains the recipe
  • The video is in English

Videos that only show cooking without verbal instructions may not work well.
